EU Regulation 2023/988 (GPSR) E-Commerce Compliance Checklist

The General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR) requires online merchants selling to EU consumers (including Shopify and WooCommerce stores) to clearly display product safety and traceability information under Articles 16 and 19.

  • Manufacturer Details (Art. 19): Legal name or registered trademark, full postal address, and electronic contact address.
  • EU Responsible Person (Art. 16): Mandatory whenever the manufacturer is established outside the EU, with an EU postal address and email.
  • Local-Language Safety Warnings: Age limits, precautions, and usage instructions in the language of each target EU market.
