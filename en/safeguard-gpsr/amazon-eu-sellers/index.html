Why Amazon Suppresses ASINs Missing EU Responsible Person Data

Regulation (EU) 2023/988 places direct verification duties on online marketplaces. In Amazon Seller Central, automated checks flag brands and ASINs across amazon.de, amazon.fr, amazon.es, and amazon.it if the EU Responsible Person contact, manufacturer address, or safety warnings are missing.

Many merchants upload compliance details inside Seller Central but leave their own WooCommerce or Shopify store exposed. Market surveillance authorities and Google Shopping inspect DTC product pages using the exact same Article 19 criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

If we resell established brands on Amazon EU, does GPSR still apply?

Yes. Every covered consumer product listing must be backed by manufacturer and EU Responsible Person details.

Do we need an EU Responsible Person if we manufacture outside the EU?

Yes, Article 16 requires an EU-established economic operator for all non-EU manufactured consumer goods sold in the Union.
