Why Manual HTML Edits in WooCommerce Fail Under GPSR

Under Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (General Product Safety Regulation), every WooCommerce store selling non-food consumer products into the European Union must display manufacturer identity, EU Responsible Person details (when the manufacturer is outside the EU), electronic contact details, model/batch identifiers, and safety warnings directly on each product page.

Pasting HTML blocks into individual Gutenberg or Classic Editor descriptions across 200 to 5,000 SKUs does not scale. Whenever a supplier changes its legal address or you switch your EU Responsible Person, manual edits leave gaps that trigger compliance flags.
