EU GPSR Rules for Importers and Dropshipping From Outside Europe

Anonymous dropshipping into the EU is no longer legal under GPSR. Understand your importer and private-label responsibilities.

Why Anonymous Non-EU Dropshipping Violates GPSR

Regulation (EU) 2023/988 fundamentally changes cross-border e-commerce from Asia, the UK, and the US into Europe. Under Article 16, no covered consumer product may be sold to EU consumers without a physical Responsible Person established inside the European Union.

Furthermore, if you import generic goods under your own private label, EU law treats you as the legal manufacturer—making you responsible for safety assessments, 10-year technical documentation retention, and full Article 19 disclosures on every listing.
