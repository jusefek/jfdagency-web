Why Toys and Baby Products Face Zero-Tolerance Enforcement

Products for children under 14 and childcare articles (cribs, teethers, strollers, educational toys, costumes) consistently account for the highest share of EU Safety Gate recalls. Market surveillance authorities and marketplaces enforce strict pre-purchase visibility for these categories.

Under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988, decisive safety warnings—such as minimum age restrictions ("Not suitable for children under 36 months due to small parts") or adult supervision notices—must be clearly visible on the online product page before checkout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is printing the age warning on the physical toy box enough?

No. For online sales, GPSR Article 19 requires safety warnings to appear on the digital product offer before the consumer buys.

Does GPSR apply to handmade wooden toys sold online in the EU?

Yes. All toys placed on the EU market must meet Toy Safety rules as well as GPSR online traceability and warning duties.
