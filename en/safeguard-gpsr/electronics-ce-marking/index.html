GPSR Compliance for Consumer Electronics, Gadgets & CE-Marked Goods

CE marking alone does not exempt hardware from GPSR online disclosures. Display model IDs, EU Responsible Person, and battery/voltage warnings.

CE Marking and GPSR Are Complementary, Not Mutually Exclusive

A common misconception among electronics, smart-home, and gadget merchants is that CE-marked hardware (covered by LVD, EMC, RED, or RoHS) is exempt from GPSR. In reality, Regulation (EU) 2023/988 layers horizontal obligations onto distance selling, accident reporting, traceability, and EU Responsible Person visibility.

Both EU marketplaces and Google Shopping require electronics listings to show the exact model identifier, manufacturer address, EU Responsible Person, and relevant warnings (lithium batteries, indoor use, voltage).

You must retain the DoC for authorities, while the product page itself must display manufacturer, EU Responsible Person, product ID, and safety warnings.

Are USB chargers and lithium-battery gadgets scrutinized closely?

Yes. Power adapters and lithium devices are high-priority inspection categories; clear voltage and charging warnings are essential.
