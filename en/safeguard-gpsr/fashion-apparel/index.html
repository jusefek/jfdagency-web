Does the EU GPSR Apply to Fashion, Clothing & Footwear Stores?

Apparel, footwear, and accessories are fully covered by GPSR. Learn what to display per brand without cluttering editorial product pages.

Why Clothing, Footwear, and Accessories Fall Under GPSR

Many fashion brands assume GPSR only targets toys or electronics. In fact, Regulation (EU) 2023/988 covers all non-food consumer products placed on the EU market unless specifically excluded—meaning clothing, footwear, handbags, sunglasses, belts, and jewelry are all in scope.

In fashion e-commerce, visual layout is critical. Article 19 allows you to present manufacturer and EU Responsible Person details inside a clean, minimalist specification box or collapsible tab without disrupting size selectors or photography.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GPSR apply to vintage or second-hand clothing?

Antiques and items placed on the market before December 13, 2024 have specific nuances, but ongoing commercial retail of new or reconditioned apparel must meet GPSR duties.

If we design clothing locally in Spain or Portugal, what do we list?

List your own company as the EU-based manufacturer/responsible entity with full postal address and customer-safety email.
