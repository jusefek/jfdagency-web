The Challenge of GPSR Compliance in Shopify Stores

Shopify merchants selling to customers in Germany, France, Spain, or anywhere in the EU must display GPSR safety disclosures directly on the product page before checkout. Putting a generic note in your footer or Terms page does not satisfy Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988.

Cluttering your main product description with legal addresses hurts mobile conversion rates, and bulk CSV imports risk overwriting variant data or formatting across Shopify Markets.

Is a footer Legal Notice enough for Shopify GPSR compliance?

No. Article 19 requires manufacturer, EU Responsible Person, product ID, and safety warnings to appear on the product offer itself.
