How Enforcement Hits Non-Compliant Online Stores

Enforcement of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 arrives through two channels simultaneously: national market surveillance authorities (connected via the EU Safety Gate portal) and automated enforcement by Amazon, Miravia, AliExpress, and Google Merchant Center.

Member state authorities can order immediate online listing removal, stock immobilization, mandatory consumer recall notices, and proportional administrative fines. Meanwhile, marketplaces proactively suppress offers lacking an EU Responsible Person.
