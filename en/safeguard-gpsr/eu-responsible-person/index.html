EU Responsible Person Under GPSR: Who Qualifies and What to Display

Clarify whether your EU importer, authorized representative, or fulfillment partner serves as Responsible Person—and display it correctly.

When Is an EU Responsible Person Mandatory?

Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 prohibits placing covered consumer products on the EU market unless an economic operator established in the Union is responsible for technical documentation and compliance tasks. If the manufacturer is based inside the EU, it fulfills this role; if the manufacturer is in the UK, US, China, or elsewhere outside the EU, an EU Responsible Person is mandatory.

The Responsible Person hierarchy is: (1) the EU-based importer, (2) a written Authorized Representative appointed by the manufacturer, or (3) an EU-based fulfillment service provider if neither of the first two exists.

Avoid Common Formatting Mistakes on Product Pages

Never use a P.O. Box as the EU Responsible Person address, and do not hide the contact email in a general About Us page. Article 19 requires the details to be visible directly on the online product offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can UK or Swiss companies act as EU Responsible Person after Brexit?

No. The Responsible Person must be physically established within a European Union member state (or EEA where applicable).

If we buy from an EU wholesaler, who is listed?

Usually the EU importer/wholesaler that placed the non-EU goods onto the Union market, or the brand appointed EU representative.
