Cosmetics Regulation 1223/2009 vs. GPSR in E-Commerce

While cosmetic formulations are governed primarily by Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, GPSR (EU) 2023/988 applies complementarily to online distance-selling disclosures, recall workflows, and any non-cosmetic items in your beauty catalog—such as LED facial masks, epilators, hair tools, makeup brushes, and travel organizers.

Multi-brand beauty stores often carry hundreds of SKUs where INCI ingredient lists are shown, yet the EU Responsible Person address, safety contact email, and usage warnings are missing from the product page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are cosmetics completely exempt from EU GPSR?

No. GPSR complements sector rules for online distance-selling transparency and recalls, and applies in full to beauty tools, brushes, and accessories.

Do warnings have to be in the customer local language?

Yes. Safety warnings and instructions must be provided in a language easily understood by consumers in the target EU member state.
