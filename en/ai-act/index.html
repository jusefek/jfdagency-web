Frequently Asked Questions About the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)

Is EU AI Act Article 4 AI literacy training mandatory for SMEs?

Yes. Since February 2, 2025, Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 requires any organization deploying or using AI tools (including ChatGPT, Copilot, or chatbots) to ensure sufficient staff AI literacy and retain audit evidence.

What does Article 50 of the EU AI Act require on websites?

Article 50 requires informing users clearly and visibly whenever they interact with an AI system (such as a support chatbot) or view AI-generated/synthetic content.

What are the penalties under the EU AI Act?

Administrative fines can reach up to €15M or 3% of global annual turnover for transparency and general obligation breaches (with proportional caps for SMEs), alongside B2B vendor qualification rejections.
