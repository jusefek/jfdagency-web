    EU Directive 2022/2555 (NIS2) B2B Supplier Qualification Guide

    Article 21(2)(d) of the NIS2 Directive mandates that essential and important entities assess supply chain security and direct supplier relationships. Procurement and CISO teams across the EU increasingly require live perimeter evidence and structured Art. 21 self-declarations before onboarding or renewing B2B vendors.

    The 10 Core Controls of NIS2 Article 21(2)(a)–(j) & Art. 23

    Legal Ref. Directive (EU) 2022/2555 Requirement
    Art. 21(2)(a)Policies on risk analysis and information system security.
    Art. 21(2)(b) / 23Incident handling and early CSIRT reporting (24h warning, 72h assessment).
    Art. 21(2)(c)Business continuity, backup management, and disaster recovery.
    Art. 21(2)(d)Supply chain security and third-party vendor governance.
    Art. 21(2)(e)Security in acquisition, development, and vulnerability handling.
    Art. 21(2)(f)Policies and procedures to assess cybersecurity measure effectiveness.
    Art. 21(2)(g)Basic cyber hygiene practices and staff cybersecurity training.
    Art. 21(2)(h)Policies and procedures regarding cryptography and encryption.
    Art. 21(2)(i)Human resources security, access control policies, and asset management.
    Art. 21(2)(j)Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and secured communications.
