NIS2 Requirements for Manufacturing Companies & Industrial Subcontractors

Automotive, machinery, chemical, and electronics OEMs are auditing tier-2 and tier-3 suppliers under NIS2. Prepare your technical dossier before your next audit.

Supply-Chain Ripple Effect Across European Manufacturing

Manufacturing of machinery, electrical equipment, motor vehicles, medical devices, and computer/electronic products is explicitly listed in Annex II of the NIS2 Directive. Large OEMs and tier-1 integrators must enforce supply-chain cybersecurity governance across their component, tooling, maintenance, and engineering subcontractors.

As a result, industrial SMEs across Europe are receiving supplier cybersecurity assessments alongside quality (ISO 9001 / IATF 16949) renewals. Failing to demonstrate basic perimeter hygiene, backup resilience, and incident escalation puts recurring production contracts at risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NIS2 apply to industrial workshops with fewer than 50 employees?

Usually not as a directly regulated entity, unless designated critical; however, tier-1 OEMs must assess their direct suppliers under Art. 21(2)(d).

Is TISAX or ISO 27001 mandatory for all manufacturing suppliers?

Only when contractually mandated by specific automotive/defense OEMs. For general industrial supply chains, structured Art. 21(2) evidence is widely accepted.

What should we send when an OEM asks for our NIS2 posture?

A dated Technical Dossier mapped to Art. 21(2), copy-paste questionnaire answers, and your 24h/72h incident reporting policy.
