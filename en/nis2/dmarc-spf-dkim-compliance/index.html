Why Email Authentication Is a Mandatory Supply-Chain Gate

When an attacker spoofs a supplier domain to send fraudulent bank-detail updates to an enterprise accounts-payable team, the resulting incident triggers NIS2 supply-chain scrutiny. Under Article 21(2)(g) (basic cyber hygiene) and (j) (secured communications), regulated EU buyers expect their vendors to enforce cryptographic email authentication.

During vendor onboarding, automated security scanners query your DNS TXT records for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. A missing DMARC record or a misconfigured SPF record exceeding the 10-DNS-lookup limit immediately lowers your vendor trust score.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a domain have two separate SPF TXT records?

No. RFC 7208 specifies that multiple v=spf1 TXT records invalidate SPF evaluation with a PermError. Always merge all authorized senders into one record.

What if we use a CRM or ticketing tool to send emails?

Every third-party sender using your domain must be included in your SPF record and configured with its own DKIM selector so DMARC alignment passes.
