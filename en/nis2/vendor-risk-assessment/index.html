NIS2 Vendor Risk Assessment: What Procurement & Risk Teams Evaluate

Whether you are assessing your own sub-suppliers or preparing to be audited by an enterprise buyer, here is how NIS2 third-party risk scoring works.

How NIS2 Formalizes Third-Party Cybersecurity Governance

Article 21(2)(d) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 requires organizations to evaluate the vulnerabilities specific to each direct supplier and service provider, as well as the overall quality of their cybersecurity practices. Regulators expect documented evidence—not informal trust.

This applies in two directions: enterprise buyers audit you as a vendor, and you must also maintain basic risk oversight over your own critical hosting, ERP, logistics, and SaaS providers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often must supplier risk assessments be updated under NIS2?

Best practice and typical enterprise policies require an annual review, or an immediate reassessment after a major security incident or infrastructure change.

Do we need to audit every minor office-supply vendor?

No. NIS2 follows a risk-based, proportional approach: focus on suppliers with network access, sensitive data handling, or operational criticality.
