How to Answer a NIS2 Supplier Security Questionnaire (Art. 21.2.d)

Enterprise procurement and CISO teams across the EU now require NIS2 security evidence before onboarding or renewing B2B vendors. Learn how to pass without stalling deals.

Why EU Enterprise Buyers Send NIS2 Questionnaires to SMEs

Under Article 21(2)(d) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2), essential and important entities in banking, energy, healthcare, transport, manufacturing, and digital infrastructure are legally required to manage cybersecurity risks across their direct supply chain. Even if your company has fewer than 50 employees and is not directly regulated by NIS2, your enterprise customers must document that their vendors meet baseline technical and organizational controls.

In practice, this means Procurement and Information Security teams send 30-to-80-question vendor assessment forms (via Excel, Ariba, or Coupa) before signing or renewing annual contracts. Vague answers or missing technical evidence routinely delay contract signatures by weeks or cost you bids against competitors who deliver structured proof in 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need ISO 27001 certification to pass a NIS2 supplier questionnaire?

Not always. Unless a specific public tender mandates accredited certification, most EU enterprise buyers accept objective external perimeter checks combined with a structured Art. 21(2) self-declaration.

What if we are still rolling out one of the requested security controls?

Always mark the control as In Progress with a documented 30-to-90-day remediation plan rather than misstating compliance. Procurement teams value transparent roadmaps.

How can a buyer verify our technical dossier is authentic?

By including a dated external audit PDF linked to a public verification page and QR code that the buyer security team can check online in seconds.
