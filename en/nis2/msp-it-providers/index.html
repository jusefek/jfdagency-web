Why MSPs and B2B SaaS Vendors Are Under Maximum Scrutiny

Managed Service Providers (MSPs), Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs), cloud hosts, and B2B SaaS platforms sit at the center of Directive (EU) 2022/2555. Because a single compromised MSP RMM tool or SaaS tenant can impact dozens of regulated clients simultaneously, enterprise CISOs treat IT and software vendors as high-risk dependencies under Article 21(2)(d).

If your own corporate domain lacks DMARC enforcement, CSP headers, or HSTS, enterprise security teams will immediately question how you secure client infrastructure.
