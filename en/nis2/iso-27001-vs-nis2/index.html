ISO 27001 vs NIS2: What B2B Buyers Actually Require From Suppliers

Does ISO 27001 automatically mean NIS2 compliance—and do SMEs really need to spend €15,000+ on certification just to keep B2B clients?

Overlap and Key Differences Between ISO 27001 and NIS2

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is an international Information Security Management System (ISMS) standard, whereas NIS2 (Directive 2022/2555) is binding EU cybersecurity law. While ISO 27001 Annex A covers roughly 75–85% of the technical and organizational controls in NIS2 Article 21(2), it does not automatically cover NIS2-specific legal obligations such as mandatory 24h/72h CSIRT reporting (Art. 23) or management-board personal liability (Art. 20).

Conversely, for SMEs acting as B2B suppliers, paying €15,000–€30,000 for an accredited ISO 27001 audit is often disproportionate when customers simply need structured proof of perimeter hygiene and Art. 21(2) alignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

If we already have ISO 27001, are we automatically compliant with NIS2?

No. You still need NIS2-specific incident notification timelines (24h/72h under Art. 23), executive accountability (Art. 20), and explicit supply-chain governance.

Can an SME pass a NIS2 supplier check without ISO 27001?

Yes, in the vast majority of private B2B contracts, provided you supply objective technical domain checks and structured Art. 21(2) policies.
