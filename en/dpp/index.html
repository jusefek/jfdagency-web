Frequently Asked Questions About EU Digital Product Passports (ESPR)

When does the EU Digital Product Passport (DPP) become mandatory?

The first mandatory milestone is the EU Battery Passport in February 2027 (Regulation EU 2023/1542), followed by phased ESPR (EU) 2024/1781 delegated acts covering textiles, footwear, electronics, furniture, and steel between 2027 and 2030.

How does ESPR DPP relate to EU GPSR compliance?

GPSR (EU 2023/988) already requires displaying manufacturer, EU Responsible Person, and safety warnings online. DPP is the natural next step: adding circular traceability via GS1 Digital Link QR codes, recycled material share, repairability scores, and end-of-life instructions.
